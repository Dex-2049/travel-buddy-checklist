Frequently Asked Questions

How do I create a checklist?

On the Home screen, tap New Checklist, enter a name, and add items using your keyboard or microphone.

Can I add items with my voice?

Yes. Tap the microphone on the checklist screen to speak items. Use the Stop button when you're finished.

How do I change quantities or mark items completed?

Use the quantity control beside an item to adjust its quantity. Tap the checkbox beside an item to mark it completed or incomplete.

How do I edit or remove checklist items?

Open a checklist and select Edit to manage the items in that list.

How do I save a checklist for later?

Save your checklist to My Checklists so you can open it again from the Home screen.

What are Reusable Lists?

Reusable Lists are templates you can use to create fresh checklists without reentering the same items each time.

How does iCloud sync work?

Easy Buddy Checklist is designed to synchronize saved checklists on compatible devices signed in to the same Apple Account with iCloud available. Ensure the devices have an internet connection and iCloud is enabled. If lists aren't syncing, contact support with the device models and software versions.

Where can I find instructions inside the app?

Tap Instructions on the Home screen for illustrated, step-by-step help with creating, using, and managing checklists.
