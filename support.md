## Frequently Asked Questions

### How do I create a new trip?
Choose one of the available Trip Templates or create a Custom Trip, then customize your trip and packing checklist.

### Can I create packing lists for multiple people?
Yes. Travel Buddy Checklist allows you to organize separate packing checklists for multiple travelers.

### Can I set reminders for items?
Yes. You can set reminders for checklist items so you don't forget important things before your trip.

### Can I save a trip for later?
Yes. Trips can be saved so you can return to your checklist later.

### Can I reuse a trip?
Yes. You can create reusable templates from your trips to make preparing for future travel faster.
