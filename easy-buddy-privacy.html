Easy Buddy Checklist

Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 20, 2026

Overview

Easy Buddy Checklist helps you create, manage, and reuse checklists on iPhone and iPad. This policy explains how information is used when you use the app, including checklist storage, optional iCloud synchronization, and voice input.

Information you enter

The app processes the checklist names, items, quantities, completion status, and reusable-list information that you enter to provide its checklist features. This information is used to display, save, and manage your lists.

Storage and iCloud sync

Checklist information may be stored on your device and, when iCloud synchronization is available and enabled, in your Apple account's private CloudKit database to synchronize your saved lists across compatible devices signed into the same Apple account. Apple operates the iCloud service. iCloud availability and privacy controls are managed through your device and Apple account settings.

Deleting a checklist in the app is intended to remove it from your saved lists and synchronize the deletion when iCloud sync is active. Apple may retain information according to its own service policies and backup practices.

Microphone and speech recognition

If you choose to add items by voice, the app requests the permissions necessary to access your microphone and perform speech recognition. Spoken input is converted into text so you can add checklist items. Depending on the speech-recognition technology used and device support, audio may be processed on your device or sent to Apple's speech recognition service for processing. Apple's privacy practices apply to processing performed by Apple.

You can choose to type items instead of using voice input, and you can manage microphone and speech recognition permissions in your device's Settings.

Sharing and third-party services

The app uses Apple platform services, including iCloud/CloudKit and speech recognition, to provide relevant features. This policy does not describe those services as private to the developer; their data handling is governed by Apple's policies. If you email support, your message and email address are received by the support mailbox so your request can be answered.

Your choices

  • Create, edit, complete, and delete checklists within the app.
  • Manage access to the microphone and speech recognition in iOS or iPadOS Settings.
  • Manage iCloud availability in your device and Apple account settings.
  • Contact support with questions about your information.

Children's privacy

The app is a general-purpose checklist utility and is not specifically directed to children. Please avoid entering sensitive personal information into checklist items or support messages.

Policy changes

This privacy policy may be updated when the app's features or data practices change. The effective date above will be updated when a revised policy is published.

Contact

For privacy questions, email travelbuddychecklist@gmail.com.
