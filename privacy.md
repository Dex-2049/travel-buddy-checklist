# Privacy Policy for Travel Buddy Checklist

**Effective Date: September 1, 2026**

Travel Buddy Checklist respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use the Travel Buddy Checklist iOS application.

## Information We Collect

Travel Buddy Checklist does not require you to create an account or provide personal information to use the app.

Travel Buddy Checklist does not collect, sell, or share your personal information with third parties.

## Trip and Checklist Information

Information you enter into Travel Buddy Checklist, such as trip details, traveler names, packing lists, checklist items, and saved templates, is used to provide the app's functionality.

This information is stored locally on your device and is not transmitted to Travel Buddy Checklist or its developer.

## Notifications

Travel Buddy Checklist allows you to create reminders for checklist items.

If you enable reminders, the app may request permission to send notifications. Notification permissions are managed through your device's iOS settings.

Travel Buddy Checklist does not use notification information for advertising or tracking.

## Analytics and Tracking

Travel Buddy Checklist does not use third-party advertising or tracking technologies.

## Children's Privacy

Travel Buddy Checklist does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

## Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.
